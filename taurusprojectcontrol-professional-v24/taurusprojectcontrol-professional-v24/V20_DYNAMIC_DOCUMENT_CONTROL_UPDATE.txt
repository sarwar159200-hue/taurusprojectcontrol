TAURUS PROJECT CONTROL — V20 DYNAMIC DOCUMENT CONTROL UPDATE

Based directly on V19/V18. Existing design and MDR 14-day logic retained.

Document Control changes:
- Pie-chart status legend is interactive. Clicking Approved, Approved with comments, Under review, Revise & resubmit or Other cross-filters the complete dashboard and live register.
- Clicking the donut center filters Approved documents.
- Review Performance weeks are clickable and cross-filter documents active in that week.
- Priority action cards are clickable.
- Overdue Aging discipline rows and age buckets are clickable.
- Discipline Health rows are clickable.
- An active interactive-filter strip shows the current chart selection and provides Clear.
- All KPI cards remain clickable with drill-down and Excel export.

New / enhanced KPIs:
- Approved with comments: count + percentage of register.
- Overdue: count + percentage of register + affected disciplines.
- On hold: count + percentage of register.
- Avg. review cycle: average days + percentage of completed cycles exceeding the 14-day target.
- Due next 7 days also shows percentage of register.

GitHub/Vercel:
- Package is kept below 100 source files.
- Vercel Root Directory: taurusprojectcontrol-professional-v20
- No new Supabase migration required for these UI/analytics changes.
